# RESEARCH-001 — Evidence Map for Ages 5–8

## Status

READY

## Objective

Build a rigorous evidence map for designing an effective learning environment for children approximately 5–8 years old.

## Research Questions

1. What developmental characteristics materially affect learning at ages 5–8?
2. Which instructional methods have strong evidence for this age range?
3. How should literacy be taught?
4. How should numeracy and mathematical reasoning be taught?
5. What approaches support science learning and inquiry?
6. What evidence exists for retrieval practice and spacing in children?
7. What evidence exists for explicit instruction, worked examples, feedback, and guided practice?
8. What evidence exists for inquiry-based and project-based learning for this age?
9. What is known about metacognition and self-regulation at this age?
10. What approaches support oral language, reasoning, communication, and vocabulary?
11. What is known about executive function and learning?
12. How can creativity and curiosity be supported without sacrificing foundational knowledge?
13. What is appropriate for practical life skills and financial literacy?
14. What does evidence say about play-based learning and its boundaries?
15. What assessment approaches best reveal genuine learning, retention, and transfer?
16. What are the risks and benefits of educational technology for children in this age range?
17. What principles should govern screen time and offline activity?
18. What child-safety and privacy principles should shape a digital learning product?
19. What existing products or models are worth studying, and where do they appear weak or differentiated?

## Required Output

Create structured research artifacts under:

docs/research/

Organize by topic rather than producing one giant undifferentiated document.

Each substantive claim should include:
- source
- evidence strength
- target-age applicability
- limitations
- practical implication

Clearly label:
- [EVIDENCE]
- [INTERPRETATION]
- [HYPOTHESIS]
- [PRODUCT IMPLICATION]
- [OPEN QUESTION]

## Source Standard

Prioritize systematic reviews, meta-analyses, high-quality peer-reviewed studies, major research organizations, government sources, and recognized educational institutions.

Use practitioner or commercial sources only when they provide useful descriptive information, not as proof of educational effectiveness.

Do not fabricate citations, statistics, study findings, or certainty.

## Deliverables

1. Developmental evidence map
2. Pedagogy evidence map
3. Domain-specific evidence map
4. Assessment evidence map
5. Educational-technology evidence map
6. Child-safety/privacy evidence map
7. Competitor/model landscape
8. Cross-topic synthesis
9. Evidence gaps and unresolved questions

## Review

After research, invoke the Red Team role to challenge:
- evidence quality
- age applicability
- overgeneralization
- contradictions
- unsupported product conclusions

Do not design the final curriculum during this task.
Do not write application code during this task.

## Completion Criteria

RESEARCH-001 is complete only when:
- the major research questions have been investigated
- evidence quality is explicitly assessed
- contradictory findings are surfaced
- limitations are documented
- implications are separated from evidence
- artifacts are organized and traceable
- red-team review is complete
- Hermes has reviewed the final output
