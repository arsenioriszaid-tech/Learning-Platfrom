# Review Protocol

Every major artifact should be reviewed from multiple perspectives.

## Educational Review

Does it support the intended learning objective?

## Developmental Review

Is it appropriate for the target age?

## Safety Review

Could it expose children to unnecessary risk?

## UX Review

Can the intended user understand and operate it?

## Technical Review

Is it maintainable and proportionate to the MVP?

## Product Review

Does it solve the defined problem?

## Verdict

APPROVED
REVISE
BLOCKED

A BLOCKED artifact cannot proceed to implementation.
