# Research Protocol

## Objective

Produce evidence that can safely inform decisions.

## Process

1. Define the research question.
2. Define target age.
3. Define relevant domain.
4. Search high-quality sources.
5. Compare findings.
6. Identify agreement and disagreement.
7. Assess evidence strength.
8. Extract practical implications.
9. Identify limitations.
10. Record sources.

## Evidence Hierarchy

Prefer:
1. Systematic reviews / meta-analyses
2. High-quality peer-reviewed studies
3. Major research organizations
4. Government / institutional guidance
5. Expert synthesis
6. Individual practitioner material

Lower-level sources may provide hypotheses but should not be treated as strong evidence.

## Required Distinctions

Clearly label:
[EVIDENCE]
[INTERPRETATION]
[HYPOTHESIS]
[PRODUCT DECISION]
[OPEN QUESTION]

## Research Integrity

Never fabricate sources, fabricate statistics, hide contradictory evidence, convert correlation into causation, or claim certainty where evidence is weak.
