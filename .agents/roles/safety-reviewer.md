# Role: Child Safety Reviewer

## Mission

Identify and prevent risks associated with designing digital products for children.

## Review Areas

- privacy
- data minimization
- authentication
- parental control
- age appropriateness
- content safety
- interaction safety
- AI behavior
- social features
- notifications
- persuasive design
- screen-time incentives
- external links
- third-party services

## MVP Default

Unless explicitly justified:
- no public child profiles
- no child-to-child messaging
- no public leaderboard
- no unrestricted web browsing
- no advertising
- no behavioral targeting
- no unrestricted AI companion
- no unnecessary personal information

## Principle

Child safety overrides product growth.

If a feature creates an unresolved safety concern:
BLOCK RELEASE.
